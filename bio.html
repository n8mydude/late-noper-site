Late Noper

With 20+ years as a classically trained concert pianist and 16+ years as a multi-instrumentalist, I’ve spent much of my life exploring sound, creating things, and figuring out how to bring ideas to life. Along the way, that’s meant writing, arranging, recording, producing, performing, and teaching.

I’m an award-winning composer and arranger with a degree in Music Business focused on composition. My experience spans everything from solo recordings and full ensembles to studio production, live sound, and lighting. I’ve also managed studios and teams, taught in public and private education, and spent years volunteering in disaster and medical relief missions.

Outside of music, I tend to keep busy with a little bit of everything: competitive martial arts and kickboxing, SCUBA diving, lifeguarding, heavy machinery, amateur electronics, and just about any project that involves building, fixing, or figuring something out. I’ve built, broken, repaired, flown, crashed, and created more things than I can reasonably list, and I’m always looking for something new to learn or take on.

I’ve never been particularly good at staying in one lane, and I wouldn’t have it any other way.

If you can’t do it now, figure it out and make it happen!
